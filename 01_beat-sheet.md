# Beat Sheet: *Right Here With You*

**Status:** v1.1 DRAFT, awaiting Daimon's approval (Gate 2 reopened 2026-10-19)
**Gate:** 2 of 7. Built from `00_story-bible.md` v1.0. The bible wins any conflict.

## How to read this file
- **28 interior pages**, each an 8×8 in frame. Front matter (cover, title page, dedication) is separate and not counted.
- **Text** is draft wording; the polished version is in `02_manuscript.md`.
- **Text side** = where the text block sits in the upper half (L or R). The art must leave that corner open.
- **Fade** lists each character's step on that page (bible §5.4): G0 full color → G3 nearly see-through. `G1→G2` means it changes during the page; GIMP shows the end state. A dash means the character isn't on the page.
- **Glow** (bible §5.3): Milo's heart bloom is on **every page** at the same strength. Noah's and Boris's appear **only on pages where they're at G1–G3**. Mom's is never shown.
- **Art refs** name the character reference to composite (files in `03_characters/`).

## Setup

- **Setting:** living room. Milo at the upright piano, playing with big happy motion. Boris sitting on top of the piano, tail curled over the edge.
- **Fade:** Milo G0, Boris G0.
- **Text side:** L
- **Text:** *This is Milo. Milo loved to get things just right.* SFX: *plink plink plunk*
- **Art refs:** Milo joyful grin (set 1 #1), seated at piano (new pose, set 3); Boris sitting (Boris set 2 #6).

### Page 2: PLONK
- **Setting:** same. Milo's hands frozen on the keys, wincing. Boris's ears flick back.
- **Fade:** Milo **G1** (the first fade in the book; no words explain it). Boris G0.
- **Text side:** R

- **Art note:** the reader should feel something is off without being told. Keep the wince small, not a tantrum.
- **Art refs:** Milo worried (set 2 `_fixed`).

### Page 3: Boris nudges
- **Setting:** same. Boris has stepped down onto the keys and is butting his head against Milo's hand. Milo laughing.
- **Fade:** Milo **G0** (color back from connection, not from getting the note right). Boris G0.
- **Text side:** L
- **Text:** SFX: *plonk-plink* (Boris's paws). Mom (offscreen): *Park time, Milo!*
- **Art refs:** Milo joyful grin; Boris head-butt (Boris set 2 #6 variant).

## Episode 1: Fade (the basketball court, Day 1)

### Page 4: Taking turns
- **Setting:** playground half-court, autumn leaves on the blacktop. Milo lining up a shot; Noah waiting with his own ball, cheerful.
- **Fade:** Milo G0, Noah G0.
- **Text side:** R
- **Text:** *At the park, Milo and Noah took turns shooting baskets.*
- **Art refs:** Milo shooting (set 3); Noah waiting with ball (Noah poses #1).

### Page 5: CLANG
- **Setting:** the ball bouncing off the rim. Milo's arms still up, face tightening.
- **Fade:** Milo **G1**, Noah G0.
- **Text side:** L
- **Text:** SFX: **CLANG!** Noah: *My turn!* Milo: *One more!*
- **Art refs:** Milo worried; Noah eager (poses #1).

### Page 6: CLANG again
- **Setting:** another miss. Noah tapping his foot, ball on hip.
- **Fade:** Milo **G2**, Noah G0.
- **Text side:** R
- **Text:** SFX: **CLANG!** Noah: *Milo... MY turn.*
- **Art refs:** Milo scowl side-eye (set 2 `_fixed`); Noah foot-tapping (poses #1).

### Page 7: GO AWAY
- **Setting:** close on Milo, fists clenched, eyes scrunched, the jagged bubble bursting out of him. Noah at the edge of the frame.
- **Fade:** Milo **G3** (nearly see-through; his heart glow, unchanged, now stands out against the pale figure). Noah G0.
- **Text side:** L (the shout bubble fills the right half)
- **Text:** Noah: *Come ON, Milo!* Milo (SHOUT): **GO AWAY!**
- **Art note:** the most important pairing in the book: **the fade comes first, the shout second.** The reader sees the faint boy behind the loud word.
- **Art refs:** Milo furious shout (set 1 #2).

### Page 8: ...okay
- **Setting:** Noah's face falls, eyes down. Milo, still faint, already looking unsure.
- **Fade:** Milo G3, Noah **G2** (his glow now visible too).
- **Text side:** R
- **Text:** Noah (small): *...okay.*
- **Art refs:** Noah hurt, quiet (poses #2); Milo worried.

### Page 9: Noah walks away
- **Setting:** wide shot, lots of white. Noah walking off with his ball tucked under his arm, shoulders low. Milo small and faint on the court.
- **Fade:** Milo G3, Noah G2.
- **Text side:** L
- **Text:** *GO AWAY jumped out. But that wasn't what Milo meant.*
- **Art refs:** Noah walking away (poses #3); Milo standing, arms limp (set 3).

### Page 10: Alone
- **Setting:** Milo sitting under the big tree, knees hugged to his chest. A few leaves falling. Maximum white space.
- **Fade:** Milo G3. **The glow is the warmest thing on the page.**
- **Text side:** R
- **Text:** Milo (thought bubble): *See? Everybody leaves.*
- **Art note:** the fear, half-said. It's the only time it's put in words, and it's in a thought bubble, not narration. Nobody comments on the glow.
- **Art refs:** Milo hugging knees under tree (set 3).

## Episode 2: Mirror (home, Day 2)

### Page 11: VRRRMMM
- **Setting:** living room. Mom in the background vacuuming. Boris in the foreground, fur puffed, eyes huge.
- **Fade:** Boris **G1**, Mom G0. Milo not on page (or tiny at the edge, G0).
- **Text side:** L
- **Text:** *The next day, Mom got out the vacuum.* SFX: **VRRRRMMMM!**
- **Art refs:** Mom vacuuming (Mom poses #1); Boris puffed (Boris set 2 #1).

### Page 12: Under the couch
- **Setting:** Boris a silver streak diving into the dark gap under the couch. Milo running in.
- **Fade:** Boris **G2**, Milo G0.
- **Text side:** R
- **Text:** Milo: *Boris! Come out!*
- **Art refs:** Boris diving (Boris set 2 #2); Milo reaching / running (set 3).

### Page 13: HISSS!
- **Setting:** Milo on his knees, arm under the couch. A paw flashes out. Milo jerking back, holding his hand.
- **Fade:** Milo **G2**, Boris **G3** (only his eyes and paw visible, his glow showing in the dark gap).
- **Text side:** L
- **Text:** SFX: **HISSSS! SWAT!** Milo: *FINE! Stay there, then!*
- **Art note:** Milo is doing his own pattern without knowing it. Don't point it out.
- **Art refs:** Milo scowl side-eye; Boris swat (Boris set 2 #3).

### Page 14: He's scared
- **Setting:** Milo half-turned to leave, looking back over his shoulder. Under the couch: two green eyes, a faint trembling cat, his glow showing.
- **Fade:** Milo G2, Boris G3.
- **Text side:** R

### Page 15: Right here with you
- **Setting:** Milo lying flat on the rug, cheek on the floor, hands tucked under his chin, facing the gap. Not reaching.

- **Text side:** L

- **Art refs:** Milo lying on rug (set 3); Boris peering (set 2 #2).

### Page 16: Waiting

- **Text side:** R

### Page 17: Purr
- **Setting:** Boris curled against Milo's side on the rug. Milo's hand resting lightly on him.

### Page 18: Was I hissing?

## Episode 3: Notice (the basketball court, Day 3)

### Page 19: Can I play?
- **Setting:** half-court. Noah shooting alone. Milo walking up, hands in pockets.
- **Fade:** Milo G0, Noah **G1** (still a little hurt).
- **Text side:** L
- **Text:** Milo: *Can I play?* Noah: *...Okay.*
- **Art refs:** Milo hopeful (set 2); Noah shooting (poses #5 variant).

### Page 20: This time
- **Setting:** after a miss. Noah reaching for the rebound. Milo looking down at his own hands, which are fading.
- **Fade:** Milo **G1**, Noah G1.
- **Text side:** R
- **Text:** SFX: *CLANG.* Noah: *My turn—* *Milo's hands were fading. This time, Milo noticed.*
- **Art note:** **Refrain 1 payoff.** Read "This time, Milo noticed" slowly.
- **Art refs:** Milo noticing hands (set 3).

### Page 21: GO—
- **Setting:** Milo's mouth open, a small broken-off jagged bubble. His other hand is coming up to his chest.
- **Fade:** Milo G1, Noah G1.
- **Text side:** L
- **Text:** Milo (SHOUT, cut off): **GO—**
- **Art note:** the bubble is small and snapped off, like it hit a wall.
- **Art refs:** Milo furious shout (set 1 #2), mid-stop; or a new "catching it" face (set 3).

### Page 22: Breathe
- **Setting:** Milo, eyes closed, cheeks puffed, letting a long breath out. Noah watching, ball on hip.
- **Fade:** Milo G1, Noah **G0**.
- **Text side:** R
- **Text:** *Milo took a big breath in... and let it out.* Milo (small): *Stay close. I just need a minute.*
- **Art note:** he asks for what he actually wants. This is the reverse of GO AWAY.
- **Art refs:** Milo deep breath (set 3); Noah waiting (poses #1, calm).

### Page 23: Noah stays
- **Setting:** Noah sitting on his basketball next to Milo, chin in hands. Milo sitting on the blacktop. Wide shot, white space, no words.
- **Fade:** Milo **G0**, Noah G0.
- **Text side:** — (no text)
- **Art note:** the "color comes back slower than it goes" rule shown without words: Milo went G1 → G0 across two pages.
- **Art refs:** Noah sitting on ball (poses #4); Milo sitting (set 3).

### Page 24: Again?
- **Setting:** another miss, the ball bouncing away. Milo, still in full color, grinning sheepishly. Noah grinning, passing him the ball.
- **Fade:** Milo G0, Noah G0.
- **Text side:** L
- **Text:** SFX: *CLANG!* Milo: *...Again?* Noah: *Again!*
- **Art note:** **the miss doesn't fade him.** This is the payoff of P5.
- **Art refs:** Milo content/sheepish (set 2); Noah passing (poses #5).

### Page 25: I didn't mean it
- **Setting:** the two boys side by side, Milo looking at his sneakers, Noah offering a fist bump.
- **Fade:** both G0.
- **Text side:** R
- **Text:** Milo: *Noah? The other day I said GO AWAY. I didn't mean it.* Noah: *I know. Your turn.*
- **Art refs:** Milo sheepish (set 2); Noah fist bump (poses #6).

## PS: Stay (home, Day 3 evening)

### Page 26: Mom sits
- **Setting:** living room, evening light. Milo at the piano. Mom sitting beside him on the bench, hands in her lap, listening. Boris on top of the piano.
- **Fade:** all G0.
- **Text side:** L
- **Text:** *That night, Milo played his song. Mom sat beside him. She didn't say anything. She just stayed.*
- **Art refs:** Milo at piano (set 3); Mom on bench (Mom poses #2); Boris sitting.

### Page 27: Right here with you
- **Setting:** Milo has stopped playing. Mom has turned on the bench and wrapped **both arms** around him; Milo has his arms around her too, face pressed into her sweater, eyes closed. Close framing on the two of them, then **the most white space of any page in the book**.
- **Fade:** both G0. Milo's glow as always.
- **Text side:** R
- **Text:** Milo (small): *Mom? I yelled GO AWAY at Noah.* Mom (small): *Right here with you.*
- **Art note:** **Refrain 3 payoff**, and the book's physical and emotional peak. The hug must look real and reciprocal (he's holding on, not just being held), because this is where "love isn't conditional" is felt, not told. Same bubble style as p. 15. Mom's face is calm and warm, never sad or concerned.
- **Art refs:** Mom and Milo hug (Mom poses #3; hug test in `parents.md`; bible §9 item 1).

### Page 28: Still here
- **Setting:** dusk; the lamp low. Milo playing again, Mom beside him with **one arm still around his shoulders** and her cheek resting on top of his head, eyes closed. Boris curled on the piano. A few leaves outside the dark window.
- **Fade:** all G0, with a soft dusk tint over the page.
- **Text side:** — (no text). Optional SFX: *plink plink plunk* (echoing p. 1).
- **Art note:** the hug hasn't ended; it's become the way they sit. In the low light, Milo's glow is the brightest thing in the room. Same glow as on p. 1; only the light around it changed.
- **Art refs:** Milo at piano; Mom leaning with arm around him (Mom poses #4); Boris curled.

## Read-aloud notes (for the reader, not printed)
- **Pause before the page turn** on pp. 6 (to the shout), 14 (to "he's scared"), and 19 (to the miss).
- **Read the GO AWAY loud** and every "right here with you" almost in a whisper.
- **p. 20** is the climax. Read "This time, Milo noticed." slowly.
- Wordless pages (23, 28): give the child time to look. If he points at the glow, let him say what he thinks it is.

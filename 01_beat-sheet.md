# Beat Sheet: *The Happy Meter*

**Status:** DRAFT v0.1, awaiting review
**Gate:** 2 of 7. Built from `00_story-bible.md` v0.3. The bible wins any conflict.

## How to read this file
- **28 interior pages**, each one 8×8 in frame. Front matter (cover, title page, dedication) is handled separately and not counted.
- **Text** is draft wording to show the beat and the rhythm. Final wording is polished at Gate 3 (manuscript).
- **Text side** = where the text block sits in the upper half (L or R). The art for that page must leave that corner open.
- **Meters** list only what Milo can see on that page. A dash means no meters are drawn. Notation: `4→2` means the meter changes on this page; `(puff)` = squares emptying, `(sparkle)` = squares filling.
- Milo's **own** meter appears only in reflections (pp. 18, 26, 28).

## Act 1: Meet Milo and his NO

### Page 1: This is Milo
- **Setting:** living room. Milo mid-leap from couch cushion to cushion (a "floor is lava" game he invented). Boris watching from the rug.
- **Meters:** none.
- **Text side:** L
- **Text:** *This is Milo. Milo is loud. Milo is fast. Milo makes up the BEST games.*
- **Art note:** pure joy. His biggest grin in the book. It sets the baseline of who he really is.

### Page 2: Pancakes?
- **Setting:** kitchen. Mom at the stove holding a spatula, smiling. Milo at the table.
- **Meters:** none.
- **Text side:** R
- **Text:** Mom: *"Pancakes, Milo?"* Milo: **"NO!"**
- **Art note:** the NO is a big jagged bubble bursting out of Milo; he looks surprised by it himself. Boris's fur puffs up.

### Page 3: ...yes
- **Setting:** same kitchen. Milo, quiet and a little sheepish, happily eating a tall stack of pancakes.
- **Meters:** none.
- **Text side:** L
- **Text:** *"...yes." The NO jumped out before Milo could catch it. It did that a lot.*
- **Art note:** **Refrain 1 introduced.** A small, funny, warm page; nobody is upset. This is a reflex, not a crime.

## Act 2: Change 1, Struggle (the basketball game)

### Page 4: Milo's game
- **Setting:** playground, the half-court. Autumn leaves on the blacktop. Milo and Diego playing HORSE; Milo mid-shot, Diego cheering.
- **Meters:** none.
- **Text side:** R
- **Text:** *At the playground, Milo and Diego played HORSE. Milo's game. Milo's rules.*

### Page 5: New rule!
- **Setting:** half-court. Diego spinning the ball on one finger, grinning, full of ideas.
- **Meters:** none.
- **Text side:** L
- **Text:** Diego: *"New rule! Every shot has to be a TRICK shot!"*

### Page 6: NO! NO! NO!
- **Setting:** half-court. Milo yelling, fists clenched, a cascade of NO bubbles. Diego trying to talk; his speech bubble shrinks to tiny dots.
- **Meters:** none.
- **Text side:** R
- **Text:** **"NO! That's NOT how you play! NO NO NO!"** *The NO jumped out before Milo could catch it.*
- **Art note:** the loudest page in the book. Diego's shrinking bubble shows him being shouted over without saying it.

### Page 7: The first meter
- **Setting:** half-court, mostly white. Diego walking away with the ball tucked under his arm, shoulders low. Milo alone, rubbing his eyes.
- **Meters:** Diego `4→2` (puff).
- **Text side:** L
- **Text:** *Diego got very quiet. He picked up his ball and walked away. And then Milo saw something strange.*
- **Art note:** the **discovery page**. The first meter in the book. Lots of white space; one leaf falling. Pause here when reading aloud.

### Page 8: PLOT TWIST!
- **Setting:** living room, evening. Owen sprawled on the couch with a book, his black-and-white cat asleep beside him. Owen's arms flung up in excitement. Milo walking in, still glum.
- **Meters:** Owen 4. Owen's cat 2/2.
- **Text side:** R
- **Text:** Owen: **"PLOT TWIST!"** Milo: *"What's a plot twist?"*
- **Art note:** Milo now sees meters at home too. They're just there, unexplained.

### Page 9: The best part
- **Setting:** couch. Owen still reading, not even looking up. Milo leaning on the arm of the couch, thinking.
- **Meters:** Owen 4. Owen's cat 2/2.
- **Text side:** L
- **Text:** Owen: *"It's when the story suddenly goes a different way. It's the best part. You never know what happens next."* *Milo didn't say anything. But he remembered.*
- **Art note:** Owen is **not** advising Milo. He's absorbed in his book. Milo overhears an idea; nobody tells him what to do.

### Page 10: Experiments
- **Setting:** dinner table. Milo hugging Mom; Dad passing a bowl. Milo watching their meters like a scientist.
- **Meters:** Mom `4→5` (sparkle). Dad `4→5` (sparkle).
- **Text side:** R
- **Text:** *Milo started testing. "Thanks, Dad." One square filled. A hug for Mom. Another square filled.*
- **Art note:** the high-IQ page. Milo has a "figuring it out" face: narrowed eyes, tongue poking out.

### Page 11: Boris is hungry
- **Setting:** kitchen floor. Boris meowing at an empty bowl. Milo pouring cat food.
- **Meters:** Boris `1→2` (sparkle). Optional small inset or ghosted earlier state showing it drained to 1 while he meowed.
- **Text side:** L
- **Text:** *Even Boris had one! When nobody fed him, it slowly went down. When Milo filled his bowl, it filled right back up.*

### Page 12: Too much petting
- **Setting:** living room rug. Boris mid-SWAT, ears flat, a third square bulging out past his bar. Milo holding his hand, surprised. Boris bolting off the page.
- **Meters:** Boris `2→OVERFILL` (wobble lines).
- **Text side:** R
- **Text:** *Milo petted Boris. And petted him. And petted him. **SWAT!** "Huh," said Milo. "Too much is too much."*
- **Art note:** comic relief to close Act 2. Milo states the rule himself; it's his discovery.

## Act 3: Change 2, Stumble (the swings)

### Page 13: Tag vs. sky kingdom
- **Setting:** playground swings, leaves swirling. Milo running in, arms wide. Nia on a swing, smiling, a flower in her hand.
- **Meters:** Nia 4.
- **Text side:** L
- **Text:** Milo: *"Let's play TAG!"* Nia: *"Let's make the swings a sky kingdom!"*

### Page 14: This time he saw it
- **Setting:** swings. Milo mid-"NO!", but frozen, eyes on Nia's meter. Nia's smile faltering.
- **Meters:** Nia `4→3` (puff).
- **Text side:** R
- **Text:** **"NO!"** *The NO jumped out before Milo could catch it. But this time, Milo SAW it.*

### Page 15: The bad fix
- **Setting:** swings. Milo, arms crossed, scowling, plopping onto a swing. Nia looking away.
- **Meters:** Nia `3→2` (puff).
- **Text side:** L
- **Text:** Milo: *"FINE. We'll play your dumb kingdom."* *Another square went dark. That was NOT the fix.*
- **Art note:** the **stumble**. Milo is trying, but it comes out wrong. It should feel familiar without feeling shameful.

### Page 16: What's your idea?
- **Setting:** swings, quieter. Milo leaning toward Nia, softer face. Nia turning back toward him.
- **Meters:** Nia `2→3` (sparkle).
- **Text side:** R
- **Text:** *Milo took a breath. "...What's your idea?"*

### Page 17: Okay, not great
- **Setting:** both swinging high; Nia pointing at clouds; leaves drifting. Nia's smile partly back.
- **Meters:** Nia 3.
- **Text side:** L
- **Text:** *Nia explained the sky kingdom. They swung. It was okay. Not great. Squares come back slower than they go.*
- **Art note:** **Refrain 2.** An honest page; repair takes time.

### Page 18: The puddle
- **Setting:** close on a puddle beneath the swings, autumn leaves floating in it. Milo leaning over it. In the reflection, a meter floats above his head.
- **Meters:** Milo (reflection only) **2**.
- **Text side:** R
- **Text:** *Then Milo looked down. There, in the puddle, above his own head, was a meter. And it was low.*
- **Art note:** the emotional center of the book. Maximum white space. The meter appears **only in the reflection**.

### Page 19: Mine too
- **Setting:** under the big tree, leaves falling. Milo sitting alone, chin in hands, thinking.
- **Meters:** none.
- **Text side:** L
- **Text:** *When Milo yelled, everyone's squares went dark. Even his own.*
- **Art note:** a quiet page to end Act 3. No meters drawn; the thought is the image.

## Act 4: Change 3, Success (the piano)

### Page 20: Milo's song
- **Setting:** living room. Milo at the piano, playing with gusto. Diego and Nia on the rug listening. Owen on the couch with his book and his cat. Boris on top of the piano.
- **Meters:** Diego 4. Nia 4. Owen 4. Owen's cat 2/2. Boris 2.
- **Text side:** R
- **Text:** *Diego and Nia came over. Milo played his song on the piano. HIS song. HIS way.*
- **Art note:** Diego and Nia are back at baseline 4. Time has refilled them, and that shows quietly that repair happened offstage.

### Page 21: Plink plonk
- **Setting:** piano bench. Nia slides in next to Milo and plays her own notes over his. Music notes tangling in the air.
- **Meters:** Nia 4.
- **Text side:** L
- **Text:** *Then Nia slid onto the bench... and started playing along. Right over Milo's song.* PLINK. PLONK.

### Page 22: The NO wants out
- **Setting:** close on Milo. Eyes squeezed shut, cheeks puffed. A jagged NO bubble pushing up from his chest, not yet out.
- **Meters:** none (Milo's eyes are closed).
- **Text side:** R
- **Text:** *The NO started to climb. Up, up, up...*
- **Art note:** the tension page. The NO is drawn as a physical thing he's holding down.

### Page 23: Caught it
- **Setting:** Milo, eyes still closed, a small thought bubble: "Plot twist?" The NO bubble shrinking.
- **Meters:** none.
- **Text side:** L
- **Text:** *Plot twist? **This time, Milo caught it.***
- **Art note:** **Refrain 1 payoff.** Lots of white space. Read slowly.

### Page 24: It's a duet!
- **Setting:** Milo's eyes pop open, huge grin, arms flung wide. Nia delighted.
- **Meters:** Nia `4→5` (sparkle).
- **Text side:** R
- **Text:** **"PLOT TWIST! It's a DUET now!"**

### Page 25: The band
- **Setting:** whole living room in motion. Diego drumming on a cardboard box, Nia and Milo playing, Boris pouncing on the keys, Owen looking up from his book, smiling.
- **Meters:** Diego `4→5`, Owen `4→5`, Boris 2 (all sparkle). Owen's cat 2/2 (still asleep).
- **Text side:** L
- **Text:** *Diego drummed. Boris pounced. Even Owen looked up from his book.*
- **Art note:** the busiest, happiest page. Owen's cat sleeps through all of it.

### Page 26: Full
- **Setting:** close on the piano's glossy black panel. Milo's reflection grinning back, his meter full.
- **Meters:** Milo (reflection only) **5**.
- **Text side:** R
- **Text:** *Square by square, every meter filled up. Even Milo's.*
- **Art note:** visual echo of the puddle (p. 18). Same framing, opposite feeling.

### Page 27: You can SEE it?!
- **Setting:** piano bench. Nia leaning in, smiling knowingly. Milo wide-eyed.
- **Meters:** Nia 5.
- **Text side:** L
- **Text:** Nia: *"Your meter's full, you know."* Milo: *"You can SEE it?!"* *Nia just smiled.*
- **Art note:** **the mystery.** Never answered. Nia's smile should be warm and a little secret.

### Page 28: Bath time
- **Setting:** bathroom, later that evening. Milo at the mirror in pajamas, holding a toothbrush or a rubber duck. Mom's voice from offscreen.
- **Meters:** Milo (mirror reflection only) `4→5` (sparkle).
- **Text side:** R
- **Text:** Mom: *"Bath time, Milo!"* *Milo looked in the mirror and gave a little smile.* *"...Plot twist."*
- **Art note:** **Refrain 3** bookends the pancakes gag. Wry, knowing smile. The single refilling square is the last image.

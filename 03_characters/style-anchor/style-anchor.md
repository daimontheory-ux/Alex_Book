# Style Anchor

The one image that defines how the whole book *feels*. It becomes `--sref` on every later prompt.

## What we're looking for
- Loose, confident **ink lines** with varied weight. A little scratchy, not vector-clean.
- **Transparent watercolor** washes that don't fully fill the outlines (some white showing through).
- **Mostly white paper.** Scenery only suggested (a few strokes for grass, one tree).
- Soft, warm **autumn** palette: reds, oranges, mustard yellow, plus the blue of Milo's outfit.
- Expressive cartoon kid with exaggerated face and body language.
- Calm overall. Nothing busy, nothing glossy, no 3D or digital shading.

## Prompt A: kid in motion (recommended first try)
```
children's picture book illustration of a joyful 7-year-old boy leaping through a pile of autumn leaves, loose confident ink linework with varied line weight, delicate transparent watercolor washes, mostly white paper background, scenery only suggested with a few brushstrokes, soft warm autumn palette of red orange and mustard yellow, expressive exaggerated cartoon face, hand-drawn newspaper comic feel, lots of negative space, calm and clear --ar 1:1 --stylize 150 --no text, letters, words, speech bubbles, captions, signature, border, frame, panel lines, 3d, digital painting, gradient background
```

## Prompt B: quiet scene (tests restraint)
```
children's picture book illustration of a small boy sitting alone under a big autumn tree, leaves drifting down, loose confident ink linework with varied line weight, delicate transparent watercolor washes, almost entirely white paper, only the tree and a few leaves painted, soft muted warm palette, gentle mood, hand-drawn newspaper comic feel, generous negative space --ar 1:1 --stylize 150 --no text, letters, words, speech bubbles, captions, signature, border, frame, panel lines, 3d, digital painting, gradient background
```

## Tuning knobs
- **Too polished or "AI glossy":** add `--raw`, or lower `--stylize` to 50–100.
- **Not enough white:** add "vignette fading to white paper, unpainted edges".
- **Lines too thin or clean:** add "dip pen ink, brush ink, slightly scratchy".
- **Colors too saturated:** add "muted, faded, light watercolor tint".
